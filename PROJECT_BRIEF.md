# Ballarat Masonic Bushwalking Club Website Brief

## Mission
Surprise Stu's masonic brother with a great dynamic website for a new masonic bushwalking club based in Ballarat, Victoria, Australia.

## Core idea
A welcoming bushwalking club with masonic roots, open to friends, family, and pets. It should feel local, outdoorsy, friendly, trustworthy, and lightly dignified without being stuffy.

## Audience
- Freemasons in and around Ballarat
- Friends and family
- Pet-friendly walkers
- People interested in easy-to-moderate bushwalks, fellowship, and local Victorian scenery

## Site goals
- Explain what the club is and who it is for
- Make it easy to join or enquire
- Showcase upcoming walks/events
- Capture the Ballarat/Victorian bushwalking feel
- Work well on mobile
- Feel polished enough to surprise the founder

## Potential features
- Home/landing page
- About the club
- Upcoming walks/events
- Walk detail pages with distance, difficulty, dog-friendliness, meeting point, gear notes
- Gallery or trip reports
- FAQ/safety guidance
- Contact/join form
- Optional members/news updates later

## Tone
Warm, inclusive, outdoorsy, quietly masonic, community-first.

## Open questions
- Club name
- Founder/brother name if safe to include
- Lodge/region references to include or avoid
- Preferred platform/hosting
- Whether we are building a prototype, full static site, or dynamic CMS-backed site
