Inaugural Walk — Slatey Creek Campground

We’re kicking things off with a friendly inaugural walk from Slatey Creek Campground No. 1 on Saturday 9 May at 10:00 AM. This first outing is intended as a welcoming, easygoing get-together for Freemasons, family, friends and dogs, with time to enjoy the bush, fresh air and conversation.

Please bring water, comfortable walking shoes and clothing suited to the weather. Dogs are welcome but should be kept on lead or under close control as conditions require. As always, check local conditions before heading out.

  • Meeting point: Slatey Creek Campground No. 1
  • Suitable for: families, friends and well-behaved dogs
  • Bring: water, walking shoes, weather-suitable clothing, lead for dogs
  • Status: scheduled

FAQ & safety

Simple, sensible walking notes.

Do I need to be a Freemason?

No. The club has masonic roots, but friends and family are welcome.

Can I bring my dog?

On dog-friendly walks, yes — please keep dogs well behaved, under control and on lead where required.

What should I bring?

Comfortable walking shoes, water, weather-appropriate clothing and anything listed on the walk details.

What if weather changes?

Walk details may change for weather or track conditions. Check with the organiser before heading out.
