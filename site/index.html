What we’re about

Fellowship, fresh air and local trails.

The Ballarat Masonic Bushwalking Club brings together people who enjoy fresh air, good company and the landscapes around Ballarat and regional Victoria.

With masonic roots and an open, family-friendly spirit, the club welcomes Freemasons, friends, family and well-behaved pets on suitable walks.

FAQ & safety

Simple, sensible walking notes.

Do I need to be a Freemason?

No. The club has masonic roots, but friends, family and guests are welcome on suitable walks.

Can I bring my dog?

On dog-friendly walks, yes — please keep dogs well behaved, under control and on lead where required. Dog suitability should be confirmed for each walk.

What should I bring?

Comfortable walking shoes, water, weather-appropriate clothing and anything listed on the walk details.

What if weather changes?

Walk details may change for weather or track conditions. Before public launch, nominate who updates the walk status and when attendees will be notified.

Are children welcome?

Yes, on suitable family-friendly walks. Children remain under the supervision of their parent or guardian.

How hard are the walks?

Each walk should list its own pace, length and suitability once confirmed. If unsure, ask the organiser before coming along.
