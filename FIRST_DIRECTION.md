# Ballarat Masonic Bushwalking Club — First Website Direction

## Project intent
Create a polished surprise website for a new Ballarat, Victoria based masonic bushwalking club welcoming Freemasons, friends, family, and pets.

The site should feel like a real, living club from day one: warm, local, outdoorsy, trustworthy, and quietly dignified.

## Strategic positioning
**Bushwalking club first, masonic roots second.**

The masonic identity should give the site character, trust, and fellowship, without making non-masonic family/friends feel like outsiders.

Positioning line direction:
> A Ballarat-based bushwalking club for Freemasons, friends, family, and dog-friendly adventures across local and regional Victorian trails.

Possible hero line:
> Walk local. Breathe deep. Enjoy good company.

## Naming direction
Recommended default:
- **Ballarat Masonic Bushwalking Club**

Softer supporting/tagline options:
- Ballarat Masonic Walks
- Ballarat Trails & Fellowship
- Bushwalking, fellowship, and fresh air in and around Ballarat

## Visual direction
Mood:
- Victorian bushland
- heritage Ballarat
- early morning eucalyptus light
- sandstone, timber, map paper, subtle brass
- friendly community outdoors, not rugged extreme adventure

Palette:
- Eucalyptus green — `#5D7758`
- Deep forest green — `#2F4538`
- Warm sandstone — `#D8C9A8`
- Slate — `#6F7C84`
- Soft cream — `#F6F2E8`
- Antique brass accent — `#B08D57`

Typography feel:
- Warm refined serif for headings
- Clean humanist sans for body/UI
- Avoid gothic, luxury lodge certificate, or secret-society styling

Brand cues:
- subtle geometry
- restrained brass/gold accent
- compass/trail/gum leaf motifs
- avoid heavy square-and-compass use everywhere

## MVP site scope
Must-have:
- Home
- About
- Upcoming Walks
- Walk detail pages
- Join / Contact
- FAQ & Safety

Nice soon after:
- Gallery / trip reports
- Announcements/news
- Past walks archive

Avoid for MVP:
- member portal
- login system
- payment flows
- complex booking engine

## Dynamic content centrepiece
The living part of the site should be the walk/event system.

Walk/event fields:
- title
- slug
- summary
- description
- date/time
- status: draft / scheduled / cancelled / completed
- location / region
- meeting point
- map link
- distance
- duration
- difficulty
- dog-friendly
- family-friendly
- gear notes
- safety notes
- organiser/contact
- hero image

## Recommended prototype stack
Fast surprise prototype:
- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind
- seeded local data or SQLite/Prisma

Recommended approach:
1. Build a polished seeded prototype first.
2. Include 3 sample upcoming walks and 1 complete walk detail page.
3. Add a join/enquiry form.
4. Keep CMS/admin for later unless immediate editing is required.

Council note: a CMS-backed MVP is attractive, but the first surprise version should not become maintenance-heavy before the club has operating habits.

## First build milestone
Deliver a launch-style prototype with:
1. visual identity direction
2. homepage story
3. upcoming walks cards
4. one complete walk detail template
5. join/contact flow
6. FAQ/safety basics
7. mobile polish

Goal: make the founder say, “Wait, this could actually launch.”

## Key risks
- club name not confirmed
- too much masonic branding could feel exclusionary
- too little identity could feel generic
- stale walk listings would hurt credibility
- safety/liability wording needs sensible care
- overbuilding before real organiser workflow exists

## Council recommendation
Proceed with a polished, mobile-first dynamic prototype centred on upcoming walks and easy enquiry. Keep masonic cues understated, local photography strong, and the UX practical for families, friends, and pet-friendly walkers.
